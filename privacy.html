Privacy

Last updated 4 Nov 2026

Using the site

The site has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Your nationality, study level, shortlist, checklists and theme are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) so they're there next time. They never leave your device unless you export them yourself or sign up for alerts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Email deadline alerts (optional)

If you sign up for alerts we store:

  • your email address;
  • the nationality and study level you chose;
  • your shortlist (programme ids) if you asked for shortlist reminders;
  • your alert choices (weekly digest on/off and weekday, reminders on/off);
  • whether and when you confirmed, plus random one-time tokens for the confirm and unsubscribe links;
  • a record of which alerts we've sent you, so you never get the same one twice.

To stop abuse of the sign-up form we briefly keep a hashed (scrambled, not reversible) version of the IP address and email used for each attempt, deleted after 2 days.

Why: only to send the alerts you asked for. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for anything else. The site owner gets a short notice when someone confirms ("New subscriber: country, level"), without your email address.

Double opt-in: nothing is sent until you click the link in the confirmation email. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 7 days.

Who processes it: the data is stored in a Supabase database and emails are sent through Resend. Both act only on our instructions.

Unsubscribe and delete your data

Every alert email has a one-click Unsubscribe and delete my data link. Clicking it permanently deletes your subscription and your send history straight away. Your email app's built-in "Unsubscribe" button does the same. If you've lost the emails, write to the site owner from the address you subscribed with and ask for deletion.

Accuracy

Funded Abroad is a free, unofficial guide. Deadlines and rules change, so always confirm on each programme's official page before applying.
